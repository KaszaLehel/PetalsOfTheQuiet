# Petal Of The Quiet

[Itch.io Page](https://shadowscythe-games.itch.io/petal-of-the-quiet)

## Overview
**Petal Of The Quiet** is a 3D indie game built with Unity’s Universal Render Pipeline (URP).  
Step into a quiet, enchanted world where every sound and piece of music carries weight.  
Explore mysterious landscapes, solve puzzles, and experience how the environment gradually comes to life as you discover it.  

This is a **complete game**, designed to deliver a short but memorable experience that places the importance of music and atmosphere at its heart.

## Features
- Atmospheric, tranquil 3D world created with URP  
- Puzzle elements integrated into exploration  
- Every moment revolves around the return of sound and music  
- A brief yet deep experience that rewards curiosity and attention  
- Handcrafted environments that evolve as you progress  

## Purpose
The project was developed as an indie experiment to combine **visual atmosphere, puzzle mechanics, and musical storytelling**.  
It demonstrates how sound and environment can work together to create immersion and emotional impact.

## Notes
- Built with Unity URP for modern rendering and performance  
- Focused on **music-driven exploration** and puzzle solving
